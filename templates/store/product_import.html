Image Quality Guidelines

  • Recommended resolution: At least 1000 × 1000 px for crisp Retina displays and dial zoom.
  • Avoid Google Search Thumbnails: Do not copy image links directly from Google search grid previews (encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com) as they are compressed to ~300px. Open the source website to copy the full high-res photo.
  • Auto-Upgrading: Unsplash, SwissTimeHouse, Amazon, and Flipkart URLs are automatically upgraded to master high-definition resolutions.
  • Automatic Enhancement: All uploaded watch images are delivered with automatic WebP/AVIF compression and precision unsharp mask sharpening (e_sharpen:50) to enhance dial numerals and metal luster.
